Privacy policy

How we use your information

Last updated 12 November 2026

Rootline only collects what it needs to run free mentoring. We never sell your information, never use it for advertising, and never charge for anything.

Questions, or want to see, correct or delete your information? Email privacy@rootline.org.uk. We reply within one month.

Who we are

Rootline is a volunteer-run, not-for-profit voluntary association with educational purposes. It offers free tech mentorship to people in the United Kingdom and Nigeria. Rootline's committee is responsible for your information, acting as the "data controller" under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

If you apply to be a mentee

  • Your name, email address, country, and town or city
  • Confirmation that you are 18 or over
  • Optional: your WhatsApp number and LinkedIn profile
  • Your track, background, what you have learned so far, links to your work, your goals and your capstone idea
  • When you are free for calls and interviews, how reliable your internet is, and how you heard about us
  • Your choices about being featured publicly, follow-up contact and testimonials
  • Our interview notes, scores and decision

If you take part in the programme

  • Your mentor match, call dates and progress notes
  • Your capstone project link and demo day presentation
  • Your certificate of completion: your name, track, date and certificate ID

If you apply to be a mentor

  • Your name, email address, country, town or city, job title, employer (optional), years of experience and LinkedIn profile
  • The tracks and areas you can mentor, how many mentees you can take, and your availability
  • Your agreements and choices, such as being listed on our website

If you email us or visit our website

  • Your emails and our replies
  • Our website does not use cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. Cloudflare also briefly processes your IP address to deliver and protect the site.

Who can see it

  • Rootline's committee can see applications and programme records.
  • Your interviewer and your mentor see what they need to interview or mentor you.
  • Other mentees in your cohort see your name and WhatsApp number if you join the cohort group chat.
  • Service providers store or carry information for us under their own data protection terms:
    • Google, for forms, spreadsheets, email and video calls
    • Cloudflare, for our website and email forwarding
    • WhatsApp, for the group chat, if you join it

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, including employers, unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

Sending information between countries

Rootline works across the UK and Nigeria, so your information may be seen by volunteers in either country. Our service providers may also store it in the UK, the European Union or the United States. Where information leaves the UK or Nigeria, we rely on legal safeguards: the providers' standard contractual clauses, the UK's adequacy rules, and, for information from Nigeria, the transfer conditions in the Nigeria Data Protection Act, including your consent when you apply.

Keeping it safe

  • Only committee members and the volunteers who need it can open our records.
  • Rootline's accounts use strong passwords and two-step verification.
  • Volunteers must not copy applicants' information to personal devices or share it outside Rootline.
  • If something goes wrong with your information, we will tell you and, where the law requires it, the regulator.

Your rights

Under UK and Nigerian law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to our use of it
  • send it to you, or to someone else, in a common format
  • withdraw any consent you gave, at any time. This doesn't affect anything we did before.

Email privacy@rootline.org.uk. It's free, and we will reply within one month.

Complaints

Please tell us first and we will try to put it right. You can also complain to a regulator:

  • UK: the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • Nigeria: the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, ndpc.gov.ng

Age

Rootline is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18. If we find we have, we delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we will email you first.
