Why Rootline

Courses teach tools. People get you hired.

Our mission is to give trainees a structured path from first project to first tech job, guided by people already doing the work.

Entry-level tech roles are hard to break into. Plenty of free courses teach the tools, but most people starting out still lack three things: someone to guide them, real project experience, and a way to show employers what they can do.

Rootline gives you all three. You follow a track in your discipline, talk with a working professional every fortnight, and build a capstone project that you present at our online demo day.

How it works

Four steps, ten weeks

  1. Apply

    Fill in a short form between 3 and 15 December. Tell us your track, your goals and what you've learned so far.

  2. Have a short interview

    Shortlisted applicants have a 20-minute video call with one of our mentors between 16 and 18 December. It's a friendly chat about your goals, not a technical test.

  3. Get matched

    We match you by hand with a mentor in your field, based on your goals, availability and time zone.

  4. Build and graduate

    Fortnightly calls with your mentor, a group chat for questions, and a capstone project you present at our online demo day. Graduates receive a certificate of completion.

Tracks

Pick your discipline

Every track follows the same shape: foundations, core tools, guided practice, then a capstone. The learning material is the best free content we can find, chosen by our mentors.

Software engineering

More tracks, such as cloud, cyber security and AI, will open as mentors join.

Questions

Good to know

Does it really cost nothing?+

Yes. Rootline is run by volunteers, nobody is paid, and the programme and the learning material are free. We'll never ask you for money.

What happens in the interview?+

It's a 20-minute video call with one of our mentors. We'll ask what you've learned so far, what you'd like to build and how much time you can give. It isn't a technical test, and there's nothing to prepare beyond thinking about your goals.

Do I get a certificate?+

Yes. Mentees who complete the programme and present at demo day receive a Rootline certificate of completion with a unique ID that employers can check with us. It records what you did with Rootline. It isn't an accredited or regulated qualification.

Can I be in Nigeria and have a UK mentor, or the other way round?+

Yes. We match across both countries. London and Lagos are only one hour apart, so calls are easy to arrange.

What if my internet connection is unreliable?+

Rootline is async-first. Calls can be recorded, and most work happens in your own time, so you won't miss out if a call drops.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I apply?+

Cohort 1 suits people who've already started learning, even a little. If you haven't started yet, pick a free course in your track and apply for cohort 2.

What happens to my data?+

We only use your application to run the programme. Our privacy policy explains what we collect, how long we keep it and how to ask us to delete it.
