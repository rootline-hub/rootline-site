Code of conduct

How we treat each other

Last updated 12 November 2026

Rootline works because people give their time and trust. This code applies to everyone: mentees, mentors, committee members and guests.

Rootline is free. No one at Rootline will ever ask you for money, bank details or payment for a job, a reference or a place on the programme. If anyone does, report it to concerns@rootline.org.uk.

Where it applies

On calls, in the cohort group chat, in emails and messages, at demo day, and anywhere you represent Rootline, including on social media.

What we expect

  • Be respectful. Treat everyone with courtesy, whatever their background, experience or views.
  • Be reliable. Turn up to calls you've agreed to. If you can't make one, say so as early as you can.
  • Keep things private. Don't share other people's personal information, messages or work without their permission.
  • Give useful feedback. Be honest and kind, and focus on the work, not the person.
  • Do your own work. Capstone projects should be your own. Say clearly where you used tutorials, templates or AI tools.
  • Speak up. If something feels wrong, tell us. You won't get in trouble for raising a concern in good faith.

What isn't acceptable

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation, in person or online
  • Discrimination, including on the basis of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age
  • Unwanted sexual attention, comments or images, or any romantic or sexual approach between mentors and mentees
  • Asking for or offering money, gifts or favours in return for mentoring, referrals or places
  • Using Rootline to sell or promote paid courses, services or products
  • Sharing someone's personal information or private messages without permission
  • Passing off someone else's work as your own

Extra guidance for mentors

  • Keep contact on Rootline channels: video calls, the group chat or email. Cohort 1 is online only.
  • Only record a call if your mentee agrees first.
  • You are a guide, not a recruiter. You're not expected to find your mentee a job, and any referral is your own choice.
  • Don't ask mentees for personal documents, financial details or access to their accounts.
  • If a mentee tells you something worrying about their safety or wellbeing, tell the committee.

How to raise a concern

  1. Email concerns@rootline.org.uk. Your email goes only to the Rootline committee. If your concern is about a committee member, email one of the other committee members directly instead.
  2. We will reply within 3 days, keep your concern as private as we can, and tell you what we plan to do.
  3. We may talk to the people involved, give a warning, change a mentor match, or remove someone from the programme or from Rootline.
  4. If you disagree with a decision, you can ask the whole committee to review it.

If someone is in immediate danger, or a crime may have been committed, contact the police first: 999 in the UK or 112 in Nigeria.

Agreeing to this code

Mentees and mentors agree to this code when they apply. Breaking it may mean losing your place on the programme or your role at Rootline.
